PEWOPS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

PewOps: Arcade Combat ("the app") is an arcade shooter game created by SM Sadat ("the developer"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Short version

You can play the whole game offline with no account. The app can also connect to the internet for two things: an optional online account (cloud save and a world leaderboard), and anonymous usage statistics, which you can switch off at any time. The app contains no advertising and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Stored on your device

  • Your game progress, chosen callsign, uniform colour, high scores, unlocked missions and weapons, credits, upgrades, settings, and a randomly generated app ID.
  • You can delete this at any time from Settings → Reset all local progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Anonymous usage statistics (on by default, can be turned off)

  • What is sent: the random app ID (it is not linked to your name or phone), the app version, the platform (Android, iOS or web), your country (worked out from your network connection; your IP address is not stored), and game events such as "app opened", "game started or ended" with the mode and score, and "mission cleared".
  • Why: to count how many people play and to improve the game.
  • How to stop it: open Online account → Privacy → Share anonymous statistics and choose OFF. Nothing is sent after that.

Optional online account

  • If you create an account the app sends and stores: your chosen username, a salted and hashed version of your password (the password itself is never stored), your game progress (experience, credits, upgrades, unlocks, achievements, callsign and colours), your best scores for the leaderboard, and the times the account was created and last used.
  • Your username and scores are shown to other players on the world leaderboard. Do not use your real name or personal details as a username.
  • Delete your account inside the app at Online account → Delete my account. This removes the account, the online progress and your leaderboard scores. You can also email the contact address below to ask for deletion.

Information the app does not collect

  • No email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or exact location.
  • No advertising ID and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Who handles the data

Online data is processed and stored on Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database) on behalf of the developer, and is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Download counts of the app are read from GitHub. The data is not sold and is not shared with advertisers.

Children

The app does not ask for names, emails or other personal details. It contains cartoon-style action violence and is not directed at young children. An online account is optional.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
