PEWOPS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

PewOps: Arcade Combat ("the app") is an arcade shooter game created by SM Sadat ("the developer"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Short version

PewOps does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It works completely offline.

Information the app stores on your device

  • Your game progress, chosen callsign (player name), uniform colour, high scores, unlocked missions and weapons, and volume settings.
  • This information is saved only in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • You can delete it at any time from Settings → Reset all local progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or location data.
  • No advertising ID, analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • The app does not use the internet and requests no permissions beyond what Android requires to run it.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising or analytics services and does not share data with third parties.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It contains cartoon-style action violence and is not directed at young children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy (for example by adding advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
